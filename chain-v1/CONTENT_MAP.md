## Interaction

- → / Space / PageDown / clicker: next build, then next section (in the appendix: next exhibit). ← / PageUp: back. ↓ ↑: the same, but scroll a tall section first.
- Home: opening (reset to first build). End: the close. A: appendix. I: index. N: speaker notes. Esc closes overlays.
- Scrolling (wheel / touch): each section arrives in its resting state. Nothing advances on a timer except the 01 title sequence. Background video always runs.
- Every section has a URL anchor (`#s01` … `#a6`).
- `?present` starts in presenter mode (nothing plays until a key is pressed). `?stills` swaps every clip for its poster frame.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: removes the wipe animations only. Footage and builds behave the same.
- Speaker notes (N) render on the same screen. When the display is mirrored, the audience sees them.
